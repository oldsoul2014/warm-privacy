Privacy Policy for Warmth

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Warmth ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application, Warmth (the "App"). We built the App around a single principle: your call history and relationships belong to you alone.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Warmth does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers.

The only information the App accesses is:

  • Your device call log — accessed locally through the READ_CALL_LOG Android permission. The App reads call records such as phone numbers, call duration, call type (incoming/outgoing/missed), and timestamps solely for on-device analysis.
  • Your local analysis results — derived metrics such as contact frequency, relationship scores, and relationship tags, computed and stored locally on your device.
  • Optional user labels — if you manually mark a contact's relationship type (e.g., Family, Friend, Work), this preference is saved locally.

We do not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, location, or network state. We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics.

2. How We Use Information

All processing happens locally on your device. We use the call log data solely to:

  • Calculate relationship metrics (e.g., persistence score, contact frequency, reciprocity).
  • Generate relationship statuses and insights displayed inside the App.
  • Help you understand your communication patterns and identify relationships that may need attention.

We do not:

  • Upload your call logs to any server.
  • Use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • Share your data with third parties.
  • Process your data in the background without your interaction.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage only: Analysis results and user preferences are stored in the App's private directory and Room database on your device.
  • No cloud storage: We do not use cloud databases, backup services, or remote analytics platforms.
  • No network access: The App does not request the INTERNET permission. It cannot send data off your device.
  • Security: Data is protected by Android's application sandbox. Other apps cannot access Warmth's private files without root access to your device.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention period: Data is retained only as long as the App is installed on your device. It is automatically removed when you uninstall the App.
  • User deletion: You can permanently delete all locally stored analysis data at any time from Settings > Clear all data inside the App.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the App removes all databases, analysis files, and user preferences from your device.

5. Permissions

The App requests the following permission:

  • READ_CALL_LOG: Required to read your call history for local relationship analysis. This permission is used only for this purpose. Without it, the App cannot perform its core analysis, although an optional manual CSV import path is provided as a fallback.

You can revoke this permission at any time through your device settings (Settings > Apps > Warmth > Permissions). Revoking the permission will prevent the App from reading new call logs and refreshing analyses, but previously computed local results remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.

6. Third-Party Services

Warmth does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, authentication, or cloud services. No data is shared with Google, Facebook, analytics providers, advertisers, or any other external party.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, please contact us and we will assist with removing any locally stored data.

8. Your Rights

Because all data is processed and stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over it. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access your locally stored analysis results through the App interface.
  • Export your data where the App provides an export feature.
  • Delete all data via the in-app "Clear all data" option or by uninstalling the App.
  • Withdraw consent by revoking the READ_CALL_LOG permission in system settings.

We do not hold copies of your data, so we cannot access, modify, or delete it on your behalf.

9. International Users

The App is designed to operate entirely offline. Because no personal data leaves your device, cross-border data transfers do not occur.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted within the App under Settings > Privacy Policy and will take effect immediately upon posting. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
