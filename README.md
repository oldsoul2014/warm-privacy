## About Warm

Warm is a fully offline call log analyzer that helps you understand your relationships through call patterns. All data is processed locally on your device — no data ever leaves your phone.

## Key Privacy Points

- ✅ **100% offline** — no INTERNET permission
- ✅ **No data collection** — all processing happens on-device
- ✅ **No third-party services** — no analytics, ads, or crash reporting
- ✅ **Local storage only** — data is deleted when you uninstall
- ✅ **User control** — you can clear all data anytime
